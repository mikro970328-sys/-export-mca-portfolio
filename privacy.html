Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026

Export MCA LLC ("Export MCA," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, company name, email address, phone number, shipping information, container number, tracking information, and other information necessary to provide export, logistics, and customer support services.

How We Use Information

We use this information to provide shipment tracking updates, respond to inquiries, deliver logistics and export services, improve customer support, prevent fraud, and comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

SMS and WhatsApp Communications

Customers who opt in may receive shipment and account-related notifications concerning active shipments, including container status, shipment milestones, estimated arrival updates, delivery notices, and other transactional communications.

Message frequency varies based on shipment activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for assistance.

Mobile Information and Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared only with service providers as necessary to operate our systems, deliver requested services, comply with law, or protect our rights.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, satisfy legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Your Choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of certain personal information by contacting us. You may also opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP.
